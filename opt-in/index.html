Operated by Kenneth Young, sole proprietor doing business as Elite Edge Analytics. This page is the public Call-to-Action / opt-in evidence for A2P 10DLC reviewers.

How consent is collected

The only subscriber is the account owner (Kenneth Young). There is no public consumer sign-up and no marketing list. He opts in inside his private Jarvis system settings by:

  1. Entering his own mobile phone number.
  2. Checking an unchecked-by-default consent box to receive automated texts from Jarvis Personal Alerts.
  3. Saving the settings (active consent required; the box is never pre-checked).

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Terms: SMS Terms. Privacy: Privacy Policy (states mobile numbers / opt-in data are not shared with third parties).
