SMS Terms and Conditions: Jarvis Personal Alerts

Effective October 8, 2026. Operated by Kenneth Young, a sole proprietor doing business as Elite Edge Analytics.

Program description

Jarvis Personal Alerts sends automated personal alerts and reminders, such as schedule reminders, briefing summaries and account notifications, from the account owner's private assistant system to the account owner's own mobile phone. It sends no marketing messages.

How you opt in

The only subscriber is the account owner. He opts in by entering his own mobile number in his private Jarvis system settings and checking a box that consents to receive automated text messages from Jarvis Personal Alerts. There is no public sign-up.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time; you will get one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or email ken@elite-edge-analytics.com.
